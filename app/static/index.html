ℹ️ About the Project & Threat Model

System engineering, dataset provenance, and operational boundaries.

🔬 Dataset Provenance

  • Source: Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity — CICIDS2017
  • Files Analyzed:
    • Friday-WorkingHours-Afternoon-DDos.pcap_ISCX.csv
    • Friday-WorkingHours-Afternoon-PortScan.pcap_ISCX.csv
    • Friday-WorkingHours-Morning.pcap_ISCX.csv (Botnet)
    • Thursday-WorkingHours-Afternoon-Infilteration.pcap_ISCX.csv
  • Total Cleaned Flows: ~990,000 across 79 raw telemetry indicators

🛡️ Known Limitations & Honest Disclosure

⚠️ Infiltration Class Imbalance: The Infiltration class possesses only ~36 ground-truth records in CICIDS2017. Even with stratified sampling, evaluation metrics on Infiltration exhibit high statistical variance and should be interpreted as exploratory.
🔐 Encrypted Payloads: As transport protocols transition towards TLS 1.3 and QUIC (HTTP/3), classification relies purely on statistical flow characteristics rather than deep packet inspection (DPI).
